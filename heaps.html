Heaps & Priority Queues.

A binary heap is a complete binary tree stored compactly in an array. The invariant is local: each parent dominates its children in a min-heap or max-heap.

parent(i) = floor((i-1)/2)
left(i) = 2i+1
right(i) = 2i+2
IQ 200: The heap is not globally sorted. Only parent-child dominance is guaranteed. That is enough to expose the minimum or maximum at the root.
