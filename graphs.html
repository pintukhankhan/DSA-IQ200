06 · STRUCTURE

Graphs — model relationships.

Graphs represent arbitrary connections. The hard part is choosing the representation and invariant that matches the problem.

Adjacency list

Space O(V+E). Excellent for sparse graphs and neighbor iteration.

Adjacency matrix

Space O(V²). Edge existence check is O(1), useful for dense graphs.

Traversal

BFS explores by distance layers. DFS explores a path deeply before backtracking.

IQ 200: Dijkstra is not “BFS with weights.” Its correctness depends on the greedy fact that the smallest tentative distance is final when edge weights are non-negative.
